How it plays

Small levels. Big fizz.

Every level is a bite-sized puzzle you can finish on a coffee break, and every world adds a brand-new twist.

Easy to pick up

Two taps and you're playing

No fiddly joysticks. Pip goes where you point and pods go where Pip stands.

  • 1Tap a tile to walk there
  • 2Double-tap to drop a fizzy pod
  • 3Pop every critter, find the exit, earn three stars
