Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Boomlets is a game for Android made by Manpreet Gugga (publishing as Kpstn Studios). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short: there are no accounts, and the game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your progress is saved only on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sends anonymous play statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase. You can turn both off in the game.

Information saved on your phone

Your stars, best times, the last world you played and your sound, music and privacy settings are saved in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Boomlets is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear now and then between levels, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for an extra life. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads SDK collects:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address (used for an approximate location, such as your country),
  • device information, such as model, operating system version and language,
  • how you interact with ads.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form the first time you open it, and you can change your choice at any time under Privacy > Ad choices on the main menu. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Google's use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Play statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To learn which levels are too hard or too easy and to fix problems, the game sends anonymous events to Google Firebase Analytics:

  • a level was started or finished (world and level number, stars, time taken, number of tries, and why a level was lost),
  • a rewarded ad was watched,
  • app opens and session length, plus device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate country.

These events are tied to a random app-instance ID, not to you. Collection is off when the game starts. It is switched on only where no consent is needed, or after you agree in the consent form. You can turn it off at any time under Privacy > Play stats on the main menu. Google keeps this data for a limited period and then deletes it. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • where in the game's code the crash happened,
  • device model, graphics chip and driver, operating system version, app version, free memory and whether the device is rotated,
  • a short list of what the game was doing just before (for example, which level was being started),
  • a random installation ID, used to count how many devices a crash affects.

Reports are used only to fix crashes, never for advertising. Crash reporting is on when the game is first opened, so a crash at start-up can still be fixed. If you are in a region where the consent form appears, crash reports then follow your choice in that form. You can turn them off at any time under Privacy > Crash reports on the main menu. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • The game has no chat, no friends list and no user-made content.

Children

Boomlets is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used the game and you want their data removed, contact us below.

Who is responsible, and why the data is used

The game is published by Gugga Manpreet Singh Karamveer, an individual trading as Kpstn Studios, Pune, India, who is responsible (the "controller") for the information described here. Contact details are under Contact below.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the legal bases for using this information are:

  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in fixing crashes, including at start-up. After the consent form, they follow your choice there.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California law), you can ask to access, correct or delete information about you, to get a copy of it, or to object to or restrict its use, and you can withdraw your consent at any time in the game's settings without affecting what was done before. Because the developer doesn't know who you are, the quickest steps are the game's settings described above, resetting your advertising ID, or uninstalling the game. For anything else, contact us and we will reply within 30 days. In the EEA or the UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

  • India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): for questions or grievances about your personal data, our grievance contact is Gugga Manpreet Singh Karamveer, at the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page. We respond within 30 days.
  • United States: we do not sell your personal information. Personalised ads from Google AdMob can count as "sharing" under some state laws; you can stop them by deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
